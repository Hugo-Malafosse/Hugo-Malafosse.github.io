About

I am a second-year PhD student at HeKA, a joint team of Inria Paris, Inserm and Université Paris Cité, based at PariSanté Campus. I am supervised by Anne-Sophie Jannot and Jean Feydy, and my work is part of the INVENTS Horizon Europe project.

Before my PhD, I studied applied mathematics at École Polytechnique and completed an M2 in Mathematics at Université Paris-Saclay. I then spent a few months in London learning about quantitative finance, before coming back to France to teach mathematics in a secondary school for a year.

Research

PhD thesis · 2025–2028
Mise au point de méthodes pour générer des patients virtuels pour simuler des essais cliniques à partir du SNDS

I develop methods to emulate clinical trials from the French national health data system (SNDS), which covers reimbursed care for almost the whole French population. My work aims to combine target trial emulation with the generation of virtual patients, using distances between treatment trajectories to compare patients over time. I am also interested in geometric approaches such as optimal transport and, more generally, geometry on the space of probability measures. The main applications are rare autoimmune and paediatric diseases.
