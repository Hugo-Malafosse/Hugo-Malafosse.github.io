About

I am a second-year PhD student at HeKA, a joint team of Inria Paris, Inserm and Université Paris Cité, based at PariSanté Campus. I am supervised by Anne-Sophie Jannot and Jean Feydy, and my work is part of the INVENTS Horizon Europe project.

Before my PhD, I studied applied mathematics at École Polytechnique and completed an M2 in Mathematics at Université Paris-Saclay. I then spent a few months in London to learn about quantitative finance.I then came back to france and spent a year teaching mathematics in secondary school.

Research

PhD thesis · 2025–2028
Mise au point de méthodes pour générer des patients virtuels pour simuler des essais cliniques à partir du SNDS

I develop methods to emulate clinical trials from the French national health data system (SNDS), which covers reimbursed care for almost the whole French population. My work aims to combine target trial emulation with the generation of virtual patients, and uses distances between treatment trajectories to compare patients over time. I am also interested in geometric approaches such as optimal transport or more genrally geometry on the space of probability measures. The main application is in auto-immune rare and pediatric pathologies.
